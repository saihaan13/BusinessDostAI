# Business Dost AI

**The AI Business Copilot for Indian SMEs**

Voice-first · WhatsApp-native · Zero training

## What is Business Dost AI?

Business Dost AI is an AI-powered business copilot designed specifically for Indian SMEs. It helps business owners manage customers, cash flow, inventory, sales, and logistics through a simple voice-first interface.

### Key Features

- 🎤 **Voice-First Input** – Just speak naturally
- 💬 **WhatsApp Integration** – Works where you already live
- 👥 **Customer Management** – Add, edit, track history, loyalty points
- 💰 **Cash Flow** – Track pending, overdue, and collected payments
- 📦 **Inventory** – Stock levels, reorder alerts, SKU, HSN, GST
- 📄 **Invoices & Quotations** – Professional billing and estimates
- 🚚 **Logistics Comparison** – Compare transporters (Rapido, Dunzo, VRL, etc.)
- 🛒 **E-Commerce Seller Mode** – Track Amazon/Flipkart/Blinkit/Zepto orders
- 🏦 **Bank Accounts** – Track multiple accounts and cash in hand
- 🤖 **AI Extraction** – Automatically creates customers and inventory from chat
- ✏️ **Edit Anything** – Full CRUD on all entities

## Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Node.js + Express + SQLite
- **Frontend:** Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS (single-file PWA)
- **AI:** Groq (free, 14,400 requests/day) with fallback
- **Database:** SQLite (file-based, no setup needed)
